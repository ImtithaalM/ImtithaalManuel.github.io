Control Engineering — The Kitticopter

Yes, really — this lab is themed around Orvillecopter, and the brief is to design a controller that helps a (virtual, thankfully) flying feline track a position input. I started by designing a proportional controller, then evolved it into a lead compensator to meet a tighter set of specifications: steady-state error under 8%, overshoot under 30%, and robustness to ±10% uncertainty in both the system's aerodynamic constant and the physical components used to build the controller — all within a 10V supply limit.

The build shown here is the physical lead compensator circuit, translating the theoretical design into real analogue hardware.
