EEE1006F

Water Level Warning Circuit

This EEE1006F project focused on designing and building a Water Level Detection System (WLDS) that could indicate the level of water in a tank and provide appropriate warnings under different loadshedding conditions.

The first part of the design was a digital circuit that produced three water-level outputs: HIGH when the tank was full, MED when it was half full, and LOW when the tank was empty. The second part used these conditions to create a warning system using LEDs and a buzzer.

The warning circuit had to distinguish between normal operation and loadshedding. Depending on the combination of water level and power condition, the circuit would illuminate either a green or red LED and produce different buzzer behaviour. This created six possible warning states, ranging from a green LED for a full tank with no loadshedding to a red LED with a beeping buzzer when the tank was empty during loadshedding.

An important part of the project was making realistic engineering assumptions. The brief allowed the water level to be represented using a variable resistor and loadshedding to be simulated using an LDR. The design also had to remain practical, use standard components where possible, stay within the R50 additional-component budget, and operate without programmable devices such as an Arduino or Raspberry Pi.
