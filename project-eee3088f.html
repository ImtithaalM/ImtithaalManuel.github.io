Design Principles — Power Subsystem PCB

Working in a group, we designed the full power subsystem for a mobile robot (the “MM”) in KiCad, with each member owning specific parts of the design for the individual design reports. My module needed to drive four motors bidirectionally — two brushed DC motors at up to 200 mA and two auxiliary motors at up to 500 mA each — while monitoring the battery over I²C with an INA219.

The board also charges a 1S1P battery from a 9V input across two selectable charge rates (≈200 mA and ≈600 mA), takes 9V from a USB-C host input, switches two external 1 A loads, and regulates clean 3.3V and 5V rails at 5% accuracy. A single ON/OFF switch had to cut standby draw below 30 µA while still delivering the robot's 2A peak current on demand.
