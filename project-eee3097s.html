Engineering Design — Micromouse

A hands-on robotics project focused on developing an autonomous Micromouse capable of controlled movement, sensor-based feedback and maze exploration.

What was the project?

The project was developed through a series of milestones, beginning with basic motion control and progressing towards autonomous maze exploration. The aim was to combine mechanical hardware, sensors, control systems and software into a functioning robotic platform.

The first milestone focused on moving the Micromouse a specified straight-line distance of 2 m. The system had to actuate the motors while using sensor feedback to estimate and correct the actual movement. This was verified through both simulation and a physical hardware demonstration.

What I worked on

I worked on the control and simulation side of the project, using MATLAB Simulink and Stateflow to model the robot's behaviour and develop the logic required for controlled movement.

The second milestone expanded the system into full maze exploration. This involved defining system requirements, modelling the different subsystems and developing the control logic needed for the Micromouse to explore an unknown maze.

Engineering decisions

A major consideration was the relationship between the simulated robot and the physical hardware. The control strategy had to account for real sensor feedback and movement rather than relying only on an ideal mathematical model.

Breaking the project into milestones allowed individual subsystems to be tested before combining them into a more complex autonomous system. This made it possible to identify problems with movement, sensing and control before progressing to full maze exploration.

What I took from the project

This project taught me how different engineering disciplines come together in a robotics system. A robot can have working hardware and working software independently, but the real challenge is making sensing, control and physical movement work together reliably.

It also strengthened my approach to engineering development: start with a measurable requirement, test individual behaviours, use feedback to identify errors, and gradually build towards a more complex system.
